probe_pairs.py -- the byte-exactness evidence for the extra-operand epilogues
============================================================================

Run on one GB300 (sm_103), torch 2.12.0+cu130, fp16.

    CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=<free gpu> PYTHONPATH=<repo> .venv/bin/python probe_pairs.py

`differ` counts raw byte differences against torch eager; `differ!nan` drops the pairs where both
sides are a NaN, whose fp16 payload nothing in this path pins. A spelling that must be exact has
to have `differ!nan` = 0. `_approx` (Inductor's own arithmetic), `_cheap` (the NaN-dropping
clamp) and `_fast` (the Newton divide) are carried to price the exact one against and are NOT
claimed exact.

The `--unary` numbers for an `_approx` row understate its error: the probe feeds an accumulator
already rounded to fp16, which is the complete domain for a chain that starts by rounding, and
not the domain the approximate chain actually sees inside the GEMM.

Provenance note: this table was produced before `probe_pairs.build` was refactored to call
`inductor_kernel.standalone`, so that arm 1 and the probe compile the same text. `swiglu` and
`rweight` were re-run afterwards and reproduced 0 of 4,294,967,296 exactly; the rest of the table
has not been re-run since. Re-running the whole file is about two minutes on a free device.
