
The diff explains the gap as two AMD ISA facts rather than modeling gaps: AMD computes exp / div
/ rsqrt as a software sequence where NV has a one-op ex2 / rcp, so layernorm, rmsnorm, softmax
and col_exp_sum over-split more -- excluding those four, AMD equals NV; and AMD lowers a wide
reduction as a multi-stage pipelined store -> gather -> combine whose partials sit at different
pipeline stages, an inner_tree order that needs a multi-stage reconstruction it does not have yet.

PR #2783 also notes at the top that it now REUSES the shared `bitequiv.core` extracted by
PR #2782 rather than hard-copying it, and that the AMD table above is byte-identical before and
after that switch, with NV untouched.
