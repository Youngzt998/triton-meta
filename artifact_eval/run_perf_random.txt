  nothing. torch is unconstrained and is not expected to match.
  near-floor rows are mostly launch overhead, so every ratio on them is pulled toward 1.
  calls per graph is `batch_n`: how many calls were captured into one graph, each on its
  own operand copy, with the replay divided by that. `-` is a row taken before the batch
  existed, when a 6.9 us replay floor was added whole to every call; on a small shape a
  `-` row and a batched row are not the same measurement and the `-` one reads slower.
